## What is Yuyu?

Yuyu helps communities, clubs and organisations run **free events**. You can publish an event page, let people sign up, send them tickets, check them in at the door, and follow up afterwards. You host it yourself, so your community's data stays with you.

Yuyu is built for free events only. It has no ticket sales, no ads and no tracking.

## What you can do with it

- **Publish events** with their own page, schedule, speakers and venue.
- **Take registrations** with custom forms, limited places, waiting lists and approvals.
- **Check people in** with QR tickets, even when the internet is unreliable.
- **Run hackathons** with teams, project submissions and judging.
- **Stay in touch** with email updates to your guests.
- **Follow up** with feedback forms, certificates and reports.
- **Manage your team** with roles for organisers, helpers and crew.
